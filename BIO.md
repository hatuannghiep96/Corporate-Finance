# Professional Bio — Ha Tuan Nghiep

## Introduction

Ha Tuan Nghiep is an operations and supply chain professional based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. He is currently Operations Manager at Tan Hung Firm, a family-owned food manufacturing business specialising in fermented bean curd, and an MBA candidate at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa (VEMBA 33). Fluent in Vietnamese, Cantonese, and English, he brings a cross-cultural perspective shaped by six years of operational leadership, multinational corporate experience, and peer-reviewed academic research.
