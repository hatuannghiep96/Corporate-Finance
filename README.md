Ha Tuan Nghiep is an operations and supply chain professional based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, with six years of experience across food manufacturing, multinational FMCG, and academic research. Currently serving as Operations Manager at Tan Hung Firm — a family-owned fermented bean curd manufacturer — he has directly led initiatives that doubled production throughput and reduced daily operating hours by 25%, unlocking capacity without additional headcount.

At Nestlé Vietnam, Nghiep built a cross-border communication channel between Vietnam and Japan and delivered 10 export and import projects within two months. He holds a Bachelor of Business in Logistics and Supply Chain Management (Distinction, GPA 3.3/4.0) from RMIT University Vietnam, where he published four peer-reviewed works — including two Q1 journal articles — and won the Nielsen Case Competition against national competition.

Now pursuing his MBA at Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, Nghiep is building the financial and strategic toolkit needed to scale Tan Hung Firm from an SME into a mid-to-large enterprise — and to lead that growth himself.
